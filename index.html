The ScatterFlow Pipeline

One Addon. Endless Environments.

ScatterFlow was built to remove the hassle of relying on multiple addons from different companies just to create great environments.

It brings the essential tools for building, scattering, and refining environments into one streamlined Blender workflow—saving you time, money, and frustration.

Biomes System

Instant, Production-Quality Biomes.

Skip the tedious, repetitive work of manually placing grass, rocks, and trees.

With ScatterFlow biomes, you can add complete, hand-crafted environments to your scenes with a single click.

Saving you time while bringing a higher level of detail and quality to your scenes.

Fully Pre-Configured

Every biome is a complete environment out of the box, with accurate asset layering and natural variation.

One-Click Workflow

No complex setup or trying to match individual assets by hand. Simply select your biome, click your target mesh, and let ScatterFlow build the base for you

Living Systems

Living, Reactive Ecosystems.

Procedural Wind

True realism needs motion. Choose from three directional wind modes: Performance for speed, Hybrid for balance, or Realistic for the most lifelike movement.

Fine-tune the movement in seconds with presets like Gentle Breeze, Windy, or Storm.

Note: Realistic mode is extremely performance-intensive, even on high-end hardware.

Dynamic Seasons System

Shift the mood of your scene with a single slider. Move from vibrant mid-summer greens to rich autumn colors and snow-covered winter landscapes.

Under The Hood

Advanced Scatter Engine.

And by advanced, we mean advanced.

Material Control & Colorization

Adjust your scatter materials to add realistic dryness, lawn stripes, and other surface details across your environment biomes—all from a single panel.

Integrated Decal System

Easily project decals such as graffiti, posters, signs, and more onto any surface.

Speed & Optimization

Keep your viewport running smoothly with optimization tools such as camera culling, proxy objects, viewport density overrides, and more.

Growth Behavior

Guide growth organically toward objects or repel it from selected targets.

Precision Control

Use advanced controls like curves, vertex paint, elevation masks, angle masks, and more to shape exactly where and how your assets are scattered.

Scatter Patterns

Use scatter masks to place your assets in unique patterns, shapes, and formations.

Manual Placement Mode

Take manual control when you need it. Scatter, position, and scale your assets exactly the way you want them.

Physics-Based Scattering

Use physics to create results standard scattering can’t—such as naturally stacked rubble piles, debris filling containers, or loose clutter that settles naturally on uneven surfaces.

Procedural Ivy

Procedural Ivy System

Give your scenes a sense of age and overgrowth with natural looking ivy. Choose from a range of included presets and create anything from subtle growth to rich, overgrown detail.

Easy Click-and-Draw System

Simply select an ivy preset and draw where you want your ivy to spread.

Realistic or Fast Modes

Choose high-detail realism when you need it, or switch to faster performance for lighter scenes.

Atmospheric Systems

Dynamic Weather Systems.

Blanket your scene in procedural rain or falling snow, generate accumulating snow layers, and completely transform the look of your environment in just a few clicks.

Procedural Rain System

Quickly add cinematic rain to your scene. From light drizzle to heavy storms, choose a preset to give your environment a wet, rain-soaked look.

Surface Collision

Rain dynamically collides with scene geometry, responding naturally to the shapes in your environment.

Ground Cover

Add a realistic layer of snow across your environments and scene objects.

Ripple Shader

Add Rain ripples to complement your rain system and make wet environments feel more alive

Procedural Snow System

Quickly add cinematic snowfall to your scene. From light snow to full snowstorms, choose a preset to transform your environment with a cold, wintry look.

Advanced Water

Advanced Water System.

Many environments need water, and ScatterFlow gives you a full-featured system to create believable lakes, rivers, ponds, and more.

Realistic Water

Create believable water with a realistic material featuring depth, surface movement, adjustable color controls, foam, and more.

Realistic Caustics

Natural light scattering beneath the water’s surface.

Shoreline Scattering

Scatter assets along shorelines or around objects in the water

Collision Ripples

Objects create realistic ripples across the water’s surface.

Buoyancy & Debris

Scatter floating debris such as lily pads, logs, trash, and more, all with realistic bobbing, drifting, and collision.
