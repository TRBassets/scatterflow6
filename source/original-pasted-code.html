The ScatterFlow Pipeline

Not just another scatter tool.

Most environment addons give you a brush and a library of static assets, leaving you to do the heavy lifting. We built ScatterFlow to be different.

It’s an interconnected pipeline. Instead of just spraying instances across a mesh, ScatterFlow allows you to drop entire, physics-aware biomes that react to seasonal changes, sway in procedural wind, and weather naturally under dynamic rain and snow. It is the fastest way to achieve production-level realism in Blender.

Under The Hood

Advanced Scatter Engine.

And by advanced, we mean advanced.

Material Control & Colorization

Fine-tune everything from a single panel. Adjust your scatter materials seamlessly, and inject realistic dryness and growth colors across your biomes for organic blending.

Physics-Based Scattering

Drop assets naturally using rigid body physics to create realistic piles of rubble, or accumulated leaves.

Speed & Optimization

Keep viewports smooth with auto frustum culling.

Procedural Masks

Drive organic distribution with powerful noise textures.

Precision Control

Use vertex groups, paths, and elevation masks.

Growth Behavior

Organically grow towards light sources or repel targets.

Grid Arrays

Array objects to generate complex walls or rock structures.

Integrated Decal System

Project photorealistic decals like graffiti, posters, and surface damage directly onto geometry without touching UV maps.

Altitude & Slope

Confine ecosystems based on height or angles.

Atmospheric Systems

Dynamic Weather Systems.

Instantly introduce atmospheric depth. Blanket your scene in procedural rain, layer accumulating snow, and completely change the narrative of your environment with responsive weather masking.

Procedural Rain System

Transform your scene into a wet environment instantly. Toggle between Drizzle, Steady Shower, and Storm presets with a single click.

Surface Collision

Rain collides with scene geometry dynamically.

Ripple Shader

Built-in responsive water ripples and puddles.

Accumulating Snow

Winterize your environments seamlessly. Choose from Light Snow or intense Snowstorm presets to dramatically alter the mood of your scene.

Ground Cover

Automatically maps realistic snow to mesh normals.

Fluid Dynamics

Advanced Water System.

Bring your shorelines and rivers to life. ScatterFlow includes a fully integrated, physics-driven water system that interacts with your ecosystem dynamically.

Flow Maps & Foam

Generate realistic river flows and ocean currents. Foam dynamically gathers around scattered rocks, shores, and intersecting geometry.

Realistic Caustics

Physically accurate light scattering beneath the surface.

Shoreline Scattering

Automatically distribute debris, shells, and wet sand along the water's edge.

Collision Ripples

Water realistically reacts to characters and rigid body objects.

Buoyancy & Debris

Scatter floating debris, lilypads, and logs that bob naturally with the waves.
